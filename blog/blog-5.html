5-Day Honest Itinerary & Tips

Back from Beijing • No Scams • True Experience

By Mumu's Travel Diary | China Tourism Guide

5 days in Beijing is enough and the subway is very convenient! The Dragon Boat Festival is coming soon. If you plan to visit Beijing, you can include the Dragon Boat Festival holiday! I have visited Beijing more than ten times and I have sorted out the 4 best and most essential routes. Save this guide right now to avoid traps!

Pre-Trip Preparation

Required Documents: ID card (or passport), household registration book for children. These are not only necessary for taking flights and checking into hotels, but also entry tickets for some scenic spots.

Advance Reservation: Popular scenic spots such as the Forbidden City, the National Museum, and Tiananmen Square must be reserved in advance! It is recommended to book the Forbidden City at least 7 days in advance, and try to book the morning session to get more visiting time.

Clothing Preparation: Adjust according to the season. Prepare breathable clothes and rain gear in summer, and keep warm in winter.

Sun Protection & Moisturizing: Beijing has strong ultraviolet rays and dry climate in summer. Remember to bring sun hats, sunglasses, sunscreen and lip balm.

Common Medicines: Carsickness medicine, cold medicine, band-aids, necessary medicines for chronic diseases, as well as wind oil essence and toilet water for anti-mosquito.

Beijing Transportation Guide

1. Which airport to choose: Capital Airport is close to the urban area with many flights; Daxing Airport has new facilities, you can choose it if you have enough time.

2. Urban transportation: You need to scan the code / insert coins when getting on and off the bus. Take the subway during peak hours, it is not crowded and fast.

3. Scenic spot reservation: Popular scenic spots such as the Forbidden City and the National Museum should be booked on the official website one week in advance, or you will miss them.

4. Items to carry: Do not bring reflectors to the Forbidden City and Universal Studios; do not bring power banks to Tiananmen Tower and the Memorial Hall.

5. Cats in the Forbidden City: The cats are in the Treasure Hall and other places. Do not feed human food; there is special cat food for sale.

Beijing Accommodation Advice

Urban Area: It is recommended to be near Qianmen Street, with convenient transportation and many hotel choices.

Haidian District: Suitable for travelers going to the Summer Palace, Old Summer Palace, Tsinghua University / Peking University. The accommodation around is quiet and comfortable.

Shichahai: You can visit the bar street at night and feel the charm of old Beijing!

Beijing Food Guide

⭐ Copper Pot Hot Pot: Beijing copper pot hot pot is definitely a major feature. The hot pot is especially great. Many hot pots now use electric pots, which taste much worse.

⭐ Luzhu Huoshao: It is stewed Huoshao with pork intestines and lungs.

⭐ Chaogan: Chaogan is not stir-fried, it is more like stewed. It is a thickened soup with pork liver in it. The authentic Chaogan tastes better the more you eat.

⭐ Beijing Roast Duck: It was an imperial food in ancient times. You must try it when you come to Beijing!

⭐ Beijing Tripe: It is the star of hot pot, made into Baodu, the taste is very good.

⭐ Old Beijing Zhajiangmian: I have eaten different noodles from various places, and the most impressive one is Old Beijing Zhajiangmian.

Scenic Area Notes

[1] Tiananmen: Strict security check, reserve in advance, bring ID card, no dangerous goods such as lighters, arrive half an hour early.

[2] Forbidden City: Closed on Mondays, book tickets in advance, opens at 8:30, it is cooler in the morning.

[3] Chairman Mao Memorial Hall: Closed on Mondays, properly dressed, no belongings allowed, no photos allowed.

[4] National Museum: Closed on Mondays, no alcohol, tripods, no food in the museum, there is a dining area on B1.

[5] Prince Gong’s Mansion: Closed on Mondays, buy tickets in advance, bring water and food.

[6] Badaling Great Wall: Wear non-slip shoes, bring water and food, not suitable for the elderly and children.

[7] Temple of Heaven: The Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests is closed on Mondays, last entry at 16:00.

[8] Summer Palace: You can choose to enter by boat from the zoo to experience the royal garden.

[9] Bird’s Nest / Water Cube: The night lights are very beautiful. You need to go through security checks to visit, sharp objects are prohibited, you need to buy separate tickets for Bird’s Nest / Water Cube.

Travel Tips

‼️ Tip 1: Do not go to the Meridian Gate from Tiananmen to the Forbidden City. It is recommended to go from Donghua Gate to the Meridian Gate, which can save a lot of time for queuing for security checks!

‼️ Tip 2: There is no need to buy a combined ticket for the Summer Palace. The area is really large. It is recommended to enter from the East Palace Gate, visit in turn, then climb Wanshou Mountain, and exit from the North Palace Gate, so that you can visit all the essence scenic spots.

‼️ Tip 3: It is recommended to enter the Temple of Heaven Park from the South Gate and exit from the North Gate, so that you can visit the most essence scenic spots.

‼️ Tip 4: If you want to live in a cheap and good hotel in Beijing, the first choice is Beijing budget hotels, the rooms are tidy and clean! But be sure to find a store near the subway station for more convenient travel.

‼️ Tip 5: It is recommended to take the subway when traveling in Beijing. Taxis are expensive and easy to get stuck in traffic. Moreover, many scenic spots are near subway stations, but remember to avoid travel during rush hours.

‼️ Tip 6: There are many historical and cultural scenic spots in Beijing. If you want to deeply understand the stories behind them, travelers with a budget are recommended to find an explanation. The feeling of listening is completely different from watching by yourself! Guides are recommended for the Forbidden City, National Museum and Old Summer Palace!

‼️ Tip 7: There is no need to go to Nanluoguxiang and Wangfujing for snacks. Things are expensive and not delicious. Recommended: Niujie, Guijie, Yandai Xiejie, Beixinqiao, Huguosi Snack Street.

‼️ Tip 8: Do not only visit the central axis of the Forbidden City. There are two routes on the east and west sides of the Forbidden City, with relatively few people.

‼️ Tip 9: Selfie sticks over 1.3 meters are not allowed in the Forbidden City and need to be checked in advance.

‼️ Tip 10: The time of the flag-raising ceremony is different every day. Check the time in advance, bring your ID card and arrive 2 hours early to take a place! It is not recommended to bring bags, because everyone sprints to grab the front row, and the bag storage is far away, which wastes time.
