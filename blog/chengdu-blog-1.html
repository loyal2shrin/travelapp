Chengdu 3-Day Travel Guide | Handwritten & Local Recommended

Best Itinerary, Food, Tips & Local Spots for Foreign Visitors

Author: MuMu | Source: China

🌤️ Chengdu Weather & Clothing Tips
  • July: 28°C – 35°C, very hot
  • August: 29°C – 37°C, heatstroke prevention needed
What to Wear:
T‑shirts, shorts, dresses are great. Bring a sun protection jacket for daytime outings.
🎒 Essential Packing List
  • 🪪 ID: Passport, student ID, senior ID (for discounts)
  • 👕 Clothes: Coat, long pants, shirt, comfortable shoes
  • 📸 Electronics: Phone, camera, power bank, selfie stick
  • 🧴 Toiletries: Makeup remover, skin care, towel, toothbrush/toothpaste
  • 💊 Medicine: Cold medicine, stomach medicine, motion sickness pills
✈️🚄 How to Get to Chengdu

By Plane

  • Shuangliu International Airport (closest to city)
    Metro Line 10 → Line 3 to Chunxi Road (≈40 mins)
    Taxi to Chunxi Road (≈30 mins)
  • Tianfu International Airport (more flights)
    Metro Line 18 → Line 1 (≈1 hour)
    Airport bus (24h) to Chunxi Road (≈1.5 hours)
    Taxi to city (≈1 hour, ¥200; carpool recommended)

By High‑Speed Train

Main stations: Chengdu East Railway Station (best), Chengdu South Railway Station

📍 Chengdu Top Attractions by Area
  • Jinjiang District: Chunxi Road, Taikoo Li, IFS, Jiuyanqiao, Night Jinjiang River, Daci Temple
  • Qingyang District: Kuanzhai Alley, Du Fu Thatched Cottage, Wenshu Monastery, People’s Park, Sichuan Museum
  • Wuhou District: Wuhou Temple, Jinli Ancient Street, Yulin Road
  • Chenghua District: Panda Base, Eastern Suburb Memory, Wangping Street, 339 TV Tower
  • Jinniu District: Happy Valley, Yongling Museum, Zaizixiang Street
🗓️ 3-Day & 2-Night Chengdu Itinerary
Day 1: Taikoo Li → IFS → Daci Temple → Wangping St → Dongmen Bridge → Jiuyanqiao
Day 2: Panda Base → Wenshu Monastery → Kuanzhai Alley → Kuixinglou St → Wuhou Temple → Jinli
Day 3: Eastern Suburb Memory → Jianshe Rd → Panda Tower → People’s Park → Yulin Rd → Twin Towers
🎫 Attraction Booking Guide
  • Chengdu Panda Base: book 7 days early
  • Wuhou Temple: book 3 days early
  • Du Fu Thatched Cottage: book 3 days early
  • Jinsha Site Museum: book 3 days early
  • Chengdu Museum: book 3–7 days early (free)
  • Sichuan Museum: book 3–7 days early (free)
  • Dujiangyan & Qingcheng Mountain: book online or on-site
⚠️ Chengdu Travel Avoid Scams
  • Chunxi Road & Taikoo Li are crowded; go early for photos
  • Jinli is better at night; shows cost ¥20–30
  • People’s Park: go early for lakeside seats; bring mosquito repellent
  • Avoid crowded Kuanzhai Alley; visit Paotongshu St or Kuixinglou St instead
💡 Sichuan Travel Tips
  • Huanglong Scenic Area is high altitude; oxygen is available inside
  • Costume photo with street performers is NOT free
  • Western Sichuan is cool; bring a light jacket
  • Tell the restaurant “not spicy” if you can’t eat spicy food
  • Jiuzhaigou is huge; you don’t need to visit every spot
  • Mount Emei monkeys are aggressive; don’t carry food in hand
✅ Useful Travel Advice
  • Book flights and hotels in advance; prices double last minute
  • Chengdu Museum is always busy; book early with passport
  • For a convenient trip, join a local tour (≈¥1000 includes transfer, hotel, tickets, meals)
