Beijing 5 Days 4 Nights Itinerary (2026)

A practical, no-rush travel guide for first-time visitors. All attractions require advance booking — reserve early!

Important: Book tickets 3–7 days in advance for all popular sites. Most sell out fast during peak seasons (summer, holidays).

Day 1: Forbidden City & Tiananmen Area

Forbidden City (Palace Museum)

  • Ticket: ¥40 (low season), ¥60 (peak season)
  • Hours: 8:30–17:00 (last entry 16:00); closed Mondays
  • Booking: 7 days in advance, tickets release at 20:00 daily
  • Metro: Line 1, Tiananmen West Station
  • Route: Meridian Gate → Gate of Supreme Harmony → Hall of Supreme Harmony → Hall of Central Harmony → Hall of Preserving Harmony → Palace of Compassion and Longevity → Imperial Garden → Palace of Earthly Tranquility → Hall of Union → Palace of Heavenly Purity → Gate of Heavenly Purity → (extra ticket) Palace of Longevity and Health → Six Eastern Palaces → Exit at Divine Might Gate

Tiananmen Square

  • Access: Free, open 24/7
  • Metro: Line 1, Tiananmen East Station
  • Highlights: Great Hall of the People, Monument to the People’s Heroes

Mausoleum of Chairman Mao (Optional)

  • Free: Reservation required 1–6 days in advance (no same-day booking)
  • Ticket release: 12:30 daily
  • Hours: 8:00–12:00, closed Mondays
  • Rules: No bags, liquids, or electronics. Free bag storage at the east side of the square.

National Museum of China

  • Free: Advance reservation required (up to 7 days)
  • Booking: Slots open at 17:00 daily
  • Hours: 9:00–17:00, closed Mondays
  • Metro: Line 1, Tiananmen East Station

Jingshan Park (Optional)

  • Ticket: ~¥5
  • Best for: Panoramic view of the Forbidden City from the top hill

Day 2: Summer Palace & Old Summer Palace

Summer Palace (Yiheyuan)

  • Ticket: ¥30
  • Metro: Line 4, Beigongmen Station (Exit A)
  • Hours: 7:00–18:00 (summer), 7:00–17:00 (winter)
  • Best time: Autumn (golden ginkgo trees)
  • Top spots: Long Corridor, Kunming Lake, 17-Arch Bridge, Marble Boat, Tower of Buddhist Incense, Longevity Hill

Recommended Routes

  1. Full Day: Beigongmen → Suzhou Street → Marble Boat → Long Corridor → Tower of Buddhist Incense → Boat ride → 17-Arch Bridge → Copper Ox → New Palace Gate Exit
  2. Half-Day (Highlights): Beigongmen → Marble Boat → Long Corridor → Boat → 17-Arch Bridge → Copper Ox → New Palace Gate Exit

Old Summer Palace (Yuanmingyuan)

  • Ticket: ¥10 (book 7 days in advance, release at 00:00)
  • Hours: 7:00–21:00
  • Metro: Line 4, Yuanmingyuan Station
  • Note: Ruins of the European Palaces are the main draw; skip if short on time.

Olympic Park (Bird’s Nest & Water Cube) – Evening

  • Metro: Line 8, Olympic Park Station
  • Plaza: Free access
  • Inside visit: Bird’s Nest ¥100; Combined ticket ¥158 (Bird’s Nest + Water Cube + Ice Ribbon)
  • Best: Night view (illuminated)

Other Popular Attractions (Flexible Days)

Beijing Zoo & Panda House

  • Zoo ticket: ¥15
  • Panda House: Extra fee; combined ticket ~¥19
  • Star panda: Meng Lan (Xi Zhimen San Taizi) — go early (before 9:00) to see him active.
  • Oceanarium: Inside zoo, ~¥160 (separate ticket)

Universal Beijing Resort (Optional)

  • Location: Tongzhou District (far from city center)
  • Ticket: Expensive; peak season lines 1.5+ hours per ride without Express Pass
  • Note: All day needed; skip if time is limited.

Transportation Tips

  • Metro: Best choice — cheap, fast, no traffic jams. Covers most attractions.
  • Lines & Key Sites:
    • Line 1: Forbidden City, Tiananmen, National Museum, Wangfujing
    • Line 2: Lama Temple, Confucius Temple, Gulou, Qianmen
    • Line 4: Summer Palace, Old Summer Palace, Zoo, Tsinghua/Peking University
    • Line 8: Olympic Park, Nanluoguxiang, Shichahai
  • Taxis: Avoid during rush hour; book via apps if needed.

Food Guide

Peking Duck

  • Closed-oven (traditional): Quanjude, Bianyifang
  • Recommended casual: Sijiminfu, Da Dong (upscale), Ziguangyuan
  • Tip: Book in advance; avoid tourist branches (long queues).

Hot Pot (Copper Pot with Sesame Sauce)

  • Top choices: Ya'er Liji, Nanmen Shuanrou, Beimen Shuanrou

Local Snacks

  • Lu Zhu (pork organ soup) – acquired taste
  • Bao Du (tripe)
  • Cha Gan (liver soup)
  • Old Beijing yogurt
  • Dou Zhi (mung bean juice) – try only if adventurous!

Accommodation Tips

  • Peak season: Prices rise sharply (summer, holidays).
  • Budget areas: Fengtai, Daxing (Line 4 access, cheaper than city center)
  • Convenient areas: Qianmen, Wangfujing, Chaoyang (close to metro)

General Travel Tips

  • ID: Passport required for booking and entry to all sites.
  • Discounts: Free entry for children under 1.2m and seniors over 60 (with passport).
  • Weather: Hot in summer (sun protection needed); occasional rain — bring an umbrella.
  • Walking: Beijing is a “walking city” — 20,000 steps daily is normal. Pace yourself!
Final Tip: This itinerary is relaxed, not “speed tourism.” Adjust days based on your booking times and energy level. Enjoy your trip to Beijing!
