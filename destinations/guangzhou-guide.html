GUANG ZHOU TRAVEL GUIDE

Complete & Easy Guide for Foreign Tourists | Attractions • Transport • Hotels • Food • Travel Tips

Quick Overview

Guangzhou, also known as Canton, is the capital of Guangdong and a major international trade hub. It’s famous for delicious Cantonese food, modern skyscrapers, historical sites, and a subtropical climate.

✅ Most Important: Try Cantonese morning tea (Yum Cha) – it’s a local lifestyle!

Best travel season: October–April with cool and comfortable weather.

5-Day Detailed Guangzhou Itinerary

📅 Day 1: Canton Tower & Pearl River

  • Morning Visit Canton Tower (Xiaoman Yao)
  • Afternoon Haixinsha Asian Games Park
  • Evening Pearl River Night Cruise

📅 Day 2: History & Culture

  • Morning Chen Clan Academy (Ancestral Hall)
  • Afternoon Liurong Temple & Beijing Road
  • Evening Shangxiajiu Pedestrian Street

📅 Day 3: European Style & Nature

  • Morning Shamian Island (European buildings)
  • Afternoon Yuexiu Park & Five Rams Statue
  • Evening Local food experience

📅 Day 4: Cantonese Food Tour

  • Morning Morning Tea (Yum Cha) experience
  • Afternoon Local market & food tasting
  • Evening Cantonese dinner

📅 Day 5: Shopping & Departure

  • Morning Taikoo Hui or shopping mall
  • Afternoon Buy souvenirs
  • Evening Departure

Accommodation Guide

Best Areas to Stay

  • Tianhe Central Business District
  • Beijing Road Downtown
  • Near Canton Tower

Tips for Foreign Tourists

  • Choose hotels near metro stations
  • Many international hotels available
  • Good price and high quality

Transportation in Guangzhou

1. Metro (Best Choice)

  • Fast, cheap, English signs available
  • Covers all major attractions
  • Very convenient for tourists

2. Taxis & Ride Hailing

  • Didi is recommended
  • Cheap and reliable

3. Airport & Transport

  • Baiyun International Airport
  • Metro Line 3 connects airport to city

Guangzhou Food & Cantonese Cuisine

Must-Try Dishes

  • Dim Sum (Har Gow, Siu Mai)
  • Cantonese Roast Goose
  • Wonton Noodles
  • Congee & You Tiao
  • Steamed Vermicelli Roll
🍜 Best food areas: Beijing Road, Shangxiajiu, Tianhe District

Essential Travel Tips for Foreigners

  • 🍵 Try Cantonese morning tea (Yum Cha) – a must cultural experience
  • ☀️ Guangzhou is warm – wear light clothes
  • 🚇 Metro is the best way to travel around
  • 🍽️ Food is very fresh and healthy
  • 🏙️ Very safe for solo travelers
  • 📱 Use Didi for easy transportation
  • 🆔 Carry your passport at all times
