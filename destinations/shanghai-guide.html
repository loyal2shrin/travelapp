SHANGHAI TRAVEL GUIDE

Complete & Easy Guide for Foreign Tourists | Attractions • Transport • Hotels • Food • Travel Tips

Quick Overview

Shanghai is China’s international financial center, blending historic Bund architecture, futuristic skyscrapers, traditional gardens, and world-class local food.

✅ Most Important: Book attractions like Disneyland & Shanghai Tower in advance.

Best travel season: March–June & September–December with pleasant weather.

Accommodation Guide

Recommended Areas

  • Nanjing Road / The Bund
  • Lujiazui Pudong
  • Xintiandi / Jing’an

Budget & Convenient

  • Near Metro Lines
  • Price rises in peak season
  • Book early for best deals
💡 Hotels near The Bund & Lujiazui provide the best views.

Transportation in Shanghai

1. Metro (Best Choice)

  • Fast, cheap, covers all attractions
  • English signs available
  • Support Alipay & Metro App

2. Taxis & Ride Hailing

  • Didi App is recommended
  • Avoid rush hours (7:30–9:00 / 17:30–19:00)

Shanghai Food & Local Cuisine

Must-Try Dishes

  • Xiaolongbao (Soup Dumplings)
  • Shengjian (Pan-Fried Buns)
  • Braised Pork (Hong Shao Rou)
  • Shanghai Noodles
🍜 Try street food near Yuyuan & Old Town for authentic local flavor.

Essential Travel Tips for Foreigners

  • 🆔 Passport required for hotels and tickets
  • 🌃 Night view of The Bund is a must-see
  • 🚇 Metro is the most convenient transport
  • 💳 Alipay Tour Pass works for payments
  • 📶 Prepare eSIM or portable Wi-Fi
  • 👟 Comfortable shoes for walking
