BEIJING TRAVEL GUIDE

Complete & Easy Guide for Foreign Tourists | Attractions • Transport • Hotels • Food • Travel Tips

Quick Overview

Beijing, the capital of China, blends 3,000 years of imperial history with modern city life. It features world-famous heritage sites, ancient gardens, old hutong alleys, and authentic Chinese food.

✅ Most Important: All popular scenic spots need advance online reservation. No booking, no entry.

Best travel season: April–June & September–November with pleasant weather.

Accommodation Guide

Recommended Central Areas

  • Qianmen / Tiananmen
  • Wangfujing Shopping Area
  • Dongcheng & Xicheng District

Budget Accommodation

  • Fengtai & Daxing District
  • Close to Metro Line 4
  • Great price difference in peak season
💡 Hotel prices rise heavily in summer and Chinese holidays. Book early for better price and location.

Transportation in Beijing

1. Metro (Best Choice)

  • Cheap, fast, no traffic jam
  • Covers almost all tourist attractions
  • Use transit card or mobile payment

2. Main Metro Lines

  • Line 1: Tiananmen, Forbidden City, Wangfujing
  • Line 4: Summer Palace, Zoo, Tsinghua University
  • Line 5: Lama Temple, Temple of Heaven
  • Line 8: Olympic Park, Nanluoguxiang

3. Taxi & Ride Hailing

  • Avoid taking taxi near scenic spots
  • Didi App is convenient for foreign visitors

Beijing Food & Local Cuisine

Must-Try Dishes

  • Peking Duck — World-famous Beijing signature food
  • Copper Pot Lamb Hotpot — Traditional local hotpot
  • Zhajiang Noodles — Classic soybean paste noodles
  • Old Beijing Yogurt — Sweet and refreshing dessert

Local Special Snacks

  • Baodu (Beef Tripe)
  • Chaogan Liver Soup
  • Traditional Beijing Pastries
🍜 Do not eat inside tourist scenic areas. Walk 1–2 metro stops away for better taste and lower price.

Essential Travel Tips for Foreigners

  • 🆕 Passport is mandatory for ticket booking and attraction entry
  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Free entry for children under 1.2m and seniors over 60
  • ☀️ Prepare sunscreen, hat and umbrella in hot seasons
  • 👟 Wear comfortable walking shoes — Beijing is a walking city
  • 📱 Buy a local SIM card or portable WiFi for internet access
  • 💧 Drink only bottled mineral water
