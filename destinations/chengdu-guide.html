Quick Overview

Chengdu, the capital of Sichuan, is famous for giant pandas, spicy Sichuan food, slow tea culture, ancient towns, and nearby sacred mountains.

✅ Most Important: Book Panda Base tickets 1–3 days in advance.

Best travel season: March–June & September–November with mild weather.

  • Near Metro Stations
  • Many clean hostels

💡 Stay downtown for easy access to attractions, food and transport.

Transportation in Chengdu

1. Metro (Best Choice)

  • Fast, cheap and clean
  • Covers main attractions
  • English signs available

2. Taxis & Ride Hailing

  • Didi App is highly recommended
  • Cheap and convenient for short trips

3. High-speed Trains

  • To Leshan: ~1 hour
  • To Emei: ~1.5 hours

Chengdu Food & Sichuan Cuisine

Must-Try Dishes

  • Sichuan Hotpot (Spicy)
  • Mapo Tofu
  • Kung Pao Chicken
  • Spicy Rabbit Head
  • Dan Dan Noodles
🍜 Tell the waiter "Mild spicy" if you cannot eat very spicy food.

Essential Travel Tips for Foreigners

  • 🐼 Go to Panda Base BEFORE 9 AM for active pandas
  • 🆔 Passport required for hotels and tickets
  • 🌶️ Sichuan food is spicy – ask for mild flavor
  • 🍵 Experience tea house culture in People's Park
  • 🚆 Book high-speed train tickets in advance
  • 📶 Use eSIM or portable Wi-Fi for internet
